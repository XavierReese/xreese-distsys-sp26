TCP takes significantly longer, even as package size grows. Although I am sure it would show more reliability over many tests, the difference was large.
I imagine that this is due to the way that TCP guarantees its packets have reached the destination, and its processes for resending, timing packages, etc.
This aligns with the greater ratio of a small packet size (say 8 bytes) compared to the large 4kb size. For TCP, this ratio in speed was ~197/.56 = 351, while for UDP it was 12/.087 = 137.
This could be because TCP is more impacted by number of packets than UDP is.
